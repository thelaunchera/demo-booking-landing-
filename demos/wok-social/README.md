## Design system

Original The Launch Era brand concept; not a reproduction of an existing restaurant's logo, tags, or layout.

| Token | Value | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Wok red | `#E32620` | Hero, emphasis and callouts |
| Deep ink | `#171615` | Text, contrast and stamp backgrounds |
| Acid yellow | `#FFE400` | Key label and CTA accent |
| Warm cream | `#F6F1E7` | Editorial breathing room |
| Heading | **Caprasimo** | Bold rounded editorial personality |
| UI/body | **DM Sans** | Clear supporting copy and controls |

Mini-tags use custom lightweight CSS rectangles rotated by a few degrees. They stay secondary to food photography and do not require images or a paid design tool. Responsive breaks include mobile up to 470px, tablet up to 900px, and desktop refinements.

### Future original-photography briefs

Produce **eight individual authentic food photographs**, 4:5 for menu cards, wide editorial crop for Experience and a hero with ample safe space. For each shot: professional food styling, natural details and believable textures; avoid distorted food, fake embedded typography, repetitive plates, mismatched ingredients and duplicated compositions. Replace each stock URL in-place when the original photo is approved; preserve its corresponding menu title and its accurate alt text.

## Functional UX

- All publicly visible text is in **English**.
- Landing has one useful hero CTA to the menu.
- Six menu cards with accurate current descriptions and category filters (All / Noodles / Dumplings / Small Plates).
- Tap, mouse, and keyboard-supported dish details dialog, Escape and backdrop close, and trigger focus restoration.
- Mobile navigation opens and closes; internal links target on-page sections.
- Photo error fallback, reduced-motion treatment, visible focus outlines and minimum 44px mobile filter tap targets.
- No invented restaurant reviews, hours, address, live orders, bookings, prices, or payment integrations.
- Footer credits **The Launch Era**.
