05 / THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Can I bring my own nail inspiration?

Absolutely. Bring a screenshot, save a look from our gallery, or tell us what colors you're dreaming about.

How long does a nail appointment take?

Times vary with each service and design. A classic manicure is usually quicker than detailed nail art.

Do I pay when I request an appointment?

No payment is collected through this page. Any final pricing and appointment details would be confirmed separately.

How do I know if my appointment is confirmed?

A preferred time is not a confirmed booking. The final appointment details would be sent after the studio reviews your request.
