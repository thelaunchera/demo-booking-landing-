- Brand: Seasonal Brew
- Descriptor: Coffee Flavors
- Tone: warm, refined seasonal café / editorial food photography.
- Palette: espresso #412210, chestnut #A86131, spiced caramel #D58B43, vanilla cream #FAF4E9, muted olive #85865A, parchment #DDBD91.
- Typography direction: Bodoni Moda (headlines), DM Sans (interface/body).
- Story: seasonal drinks, bakery atmosphere and neighborhood hospitality; demo photography and concept only.
