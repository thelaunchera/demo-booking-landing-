# Photo assets and provenance

This archive contains the exact 13 WebP images used by the current demo: five service photographs, five editorial gallery photographs, one hero photograph, one final-CTA photograph (reused from the gallery), and the matched before/after sink example.

The project research notes record selection from Pexels/Unsplash search results and rejection of watermarked or visibly branded candidates. Original creator/source-page links were not retained for every selected file. The cached search-media links are not included as attribution claims. Before using these photographs commercially, verify each image's original source, license, and attribution requirements; replace any image whose rights cannot be confirmed.

The before/after pair is illustrative only and is not represented as a Clara customer result. This package does not grant rights to third-party photographs.
